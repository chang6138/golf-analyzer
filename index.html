R10 Shot Log

Abnormal Shots
Shots that stand out from your usual numbers, look like a misread, or look like they were logged under the wrong club. Exclude a shot to leave it out of every chart and stat, or dismiss it once you've checked it.
Load Setting
The R10 doesn't record how loaded a swing was, so 58, 52 and PW shots are labelled 1/3, 1/2 or fully loaded by club speed. Set where each label starts here; the Shot Log and every chart follow these settings.
